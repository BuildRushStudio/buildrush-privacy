Privacy Policy

Do Not Press It

Last updated: 24 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BuildRush handles information when you use the Android game Do Not Press It.

By using the app, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information Stored by the Game

Do Not Press It stores certain gameplay information locally on your device so that your progress and preferences can be remembered.

This may include:

  • Chapter progress
  • Completed chapters
  • Lifetime button presses
  • Failures and gameplay statistics
  • Achievements
  • Sound settings
  • Music settings
  • Vibration settings
  • Other game preferences

This gameplay information is stored locally by the application and is used to operate game features.

2. Advertising

Do Not Press It uses Google AdMob to display advertisements.

Google and its advertising partners may collect or process certain information in order to provide, measure, personalise and protect advertising services.

This information may include:

  • Device identifiers
  • Advertising identifiers
  • IP address
  • General device information
  • App interaction information
  • Advertising impressions
  • Advertising interactions
  • Diagnostic information
  • Information used for fraud prevention and security

The exact information processed by Google may depend on your device, location, privacy choices and advertising settings.

3. Advertising Consent

Where required by law, including in the United Kingdom, European Economic Area and Switzerland, Do Not Press It uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and manage advertising consent.

Depending on your location and choices, advertisements may be personalised or non-personalised.

Where required, you may also be provided with an option inside the app to review or change your privacy choices.

4. Third-Party Services

Do Not Press It may use third-party services provided by Google, including:

  • Google AdMob
  • Google User Messaging Platform
  • Google Play services where required by Android

Information processed by these services is subject to Google's own privacy policies and terms.

You can read Google's Privacy Policy here:

https://policies.google.com/privacy

5. Data Sharing

BuildRush does not independently sell your personal information.

Information may be processed or shared with Google and its advertising partners where necessary to provide advertising, analytics, measurement, fraud prevention and related services.

6. Rewarded Advertisements

Do Not Press It may offer optional rewarded advertisements.

Watching a rewarded advertisement may provide an optional in-game benefit, such as additional time or another gameplay advantage.

Rewarded advertisements are optional and are not required to continue playing the game.

7. Children's Privacy

Do Not Press It is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children under the age of 13.

BuildRush does not knowingly request children to directly provide personal information through the game.

8. Data Security

BuildRush takes reasonable steps to design the application in a way that limits unnecessary collection of personal information.

However, no electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

9. Your Choices

Depending on your location, you may have rights relating to how your personal information is processed.

You may also be able to manage advertising and privacy preferences through:

  • The privacy options provided inside Do Not Press It
  • Your Android device settings
  • Your Google advertising settings
  • The consent interface provided when the app starts

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

BuildRush may update this Privacy Policy when the game, advertising services or legal requirements change.

When this happens, the updated version will be published on this page and the "Last updated" date will be changed.
